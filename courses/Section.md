You can produce professional-grade videos without buying expensive microphones, camera gear, or editing suites by using the following AI tech stacks:

* ChatGPT – Input individual sections of the syllabus to generate word-for-word lecture transcripts and voiceover prompts.
* Gamma App – Type in a module summary, and the tool will automatically design your lecture slides, complete with structural layouts and bullet points.

## 2. AI Video Avatars and Slide Presenters (No Camera Needed)

* HeyGen – Upload your slide images or script. The platform generates a highly realistic AI avatar that stands next to your presentation slides and delivers the lecture.
* Synthesia – The enterprise standard tool for technical tutorials. Converts text scripts directly into educational videos featuring diverse, lifelike AI presenters.

## 3. AI Voiceovers and Text-to-Speech Cloning

* ElevenLabs – Offers highly natural AI voice synthesis. You can type text directly into the system or record a short audio snippet of your own voice to create an exact, professional voice clone to read your slide transcripts.

## 4. AI Video Editors & Automated B-Roll Captions

* Descript – A text-based video editor. You edit your lecture video by editing the typed text transcript. It automatically removes filler words ("um", "uh"), fixes audio levels, and places dynamic on-screen code text overlays.
* Runway Gen-3 – Generates realistic, cinematic AI video clips. Use it to create visual B-roll sequences (like a robotic arm printing a rocket engine) to illustrate your complex technical explanations.
